Overview

The Overview page is your command center. It shows farm health, order volume, low-stock warnings, and recent activity in one place.

1
Review the farm snapshot — check active batches, species breakdown, and any batch alerts.
2
Watch shop metrics — see recent orders count, low-stock products, and payment health.
3
Use Quick Links to jump directly to Farm Management, Users, Content, Orders, Analytics, or Suppliers.
Nigerian farm context: If you run a poultry farm in Ogun State, the Overview might show 3 active broiler batches, 12 unread batch alerts, and 2 feed items below reorder point.

Notifications

Notifications alert you to important events across the farm and shop so nothing slips through the cracks.

1
Check the bell icon in the top bar — it shows unread notification count.
2
Filter by type — batch alerts, order updates, payment confirmations, system warnings.
3
Mark as read individually or use Mark all read to clear the backlog.
Real example: A mortality spike in Batch #47 triggers an alert: "Mortality log created for Broiler Batch #47 — 12 birds recorded on 12 Aug 2026."

Website Content

Control what customers see on the public-facing website without touching code.

1
Open Website Content from the sidebar under Management.
2
Choose a section — Hero Banner, About Us, Contact Info, FAQ, Delivery Info, Terms & Privacy, or Returns Policy.
3
Edit and save — use the rich text editor to update headings, paragraphs, and images. Changes go live immediately.
Nigerian context: Update the Hero Banner to promote "National Day Catfish Special — 20% off all smoked catfish." In Delivery Info, add "We deliver within Lagos in 24 hours and to Ibadan within 48 hours."

Farm Management

The Farm Management hub is your central workspace for everything related to livestock, poultry, and feed operations.

1
Start from the dashboard — see total/active/closed batches, feed inventory count, low-stock warnings, and recent mortality logs.
2
Navigate to sub-sections — Batches, Suppliers, Species, Categories, Feed Inventory, Health Records, Daily Activities, and Analytics.
3
Use filters and search on list pages to find specific batches, species, or suppliers quickly.
Real example: A farm manager in Kaduna filters batches by Poultry → Broiler to review all active broiler flocks before placing a feed order.

Batches

Batches represent groups of animals raised together. Track their lifecycle from intake to harvest.

1
Create a batch — go to Farm Management → Batches → Add Batch. Enter a name, select species/category, set start date and initial count.
2
Update status — mark as Active while rearing, then Closed after harvest.
3
Log daily activities — feed, growth, mortality, vaccination, and harvest records are all tied to a batch.
Nigerian examples:
• Poultry — Broiler: "Broiler Batch A — 500 birds, 28-day cycle, hatchery: Zartech Farms, Ibadan."
• Poultry — Layer: "Layer Batch B — 300 birds, ISA Brown, started 15 Mar 2026."
• Livestock — White Fulani: "White Fulani Herd C — 25 cows, grazing at Kano State farm."
• Livestock — Sokoto Gudali: "Sokoto Gudali Herd D — 18 cows, Sokoto origin, breeding stock."

Suppliers

Maintain a directory of feed suppliers, veterinary drug vendors, and equipment providers.

1
Add a supplier — go to Farm Management → Suppliers → Add Supplier. Fill name, contact person, phone, email, and address.
2
Link to inventory — when creating feed inventory items, assign them to a supplier so you know who to reorder from.
3
Keep records updated — edit supplier details if phone numbers or delivery addresses change.

Feed Inventory

Track feed stock levels, set reorder points, and ensure your animals never run out of feed.

1
Add inventory item — Farm Management → Feed Inventory → Add. Enter feed type, category, current quantity (kg), and reorder point.
2
Monitor low stock — the dashboard flags items at or below reorder point. The list view shows count of low-stock items.
3
Update quantities — edit the record when you receive a new delivery or issue feed to a batch.
Real Nigerian feed types:
• Broiler Starter — 50kg bag, brand: Top Feed
• Broiler Finisher — 50kg bag, brand: Grand Cereals
• Layer Mash — 25kg bag, brand: Vital Feed
• Maize (whole) — 100kg, local grain market, Ibadan
• Soya Bean Meal — 50kg, protein supplement

Health & Vaccination Records

Log health issues, medications, and vaccinations for every batch to maintain biosecurity and traceability.

1
Record a health issue — from a batch detail page, add a Health/Medication Log. Describe symptoms, treatment given, and outcome.
2
Schedule vaccinations — add Vaccination Records with vaccine name, dosage, administration date, and next due date.
3
Review records — use Health Records list to see all logs across batches and filter by date range.
Real Nigerian context:
• Newcastle Disease — vaccinate broilers on day 7 and day 21 using Lasota vaccine.
• Coccidiosis — treat with Amprolium in drinking water for 5 days.
• Gumboro (IBD) — vaccination at 10 days and 24 days.
• Record: "Broiler Batch A — 12 birds showed lethargy on 15 Aug; treated with Oxytetracycline; 2 mortalities."

Shop / Orders

Manage the online shop: products, categories, pricing, stock, and customer orders.

1
Create categories — Shop / Orders → Products → Categories. Examples: Catfish, Poultry, Livestock, Feed, Supplies.
2
Add products — upload images, set prices, assign categories, and manage stock levels. Use clear product names and weights.
3
Process orders — review incoming orders, update status (pending → confirmed → shipped → delivered), and mark payments.
4
Manage payments — confirm Paystack or bank transfers, handle failed payments, and export CSV reports.
Real Nigerian shop examples:
• Catfish — "Fresh Smoked Catfish (1kg)" — ₦4,500
• Poultry — "Live Broiler (1.8kg avg)" — ₦3,200
• Feed — "Broiler Starter 50kg" — ₦18,500
• Eggs — "Tray of Eggs (30 pcs)" — ₦2,800

Staff Management

Create and manage staff accounts that can access the admin dashboard. Only Super Admins and Super Staff can access this section.

1
Create staff account — Staff Management → Create Staff. Enter full name, email, phone, and assign role (STAFF or SUPER_STAFF).
2
Set permissions — Super Staff can manage regular staff but cannot touch Super Admins. Super Admins have full access.
3
Edit or deactivate — update details or deactivate accounts when staff leave. Deactivated staff cannot log in.
Role examples:
• STAFF — "Chinedu Okafor — manages daily feed logs and batch updates."
• SUPER_STAFF — "Funke Adeyemi — oversees staff, manages inventory, cannot delete users."
• SUPER_ADMIN — "TAMIPEE Owner — full system access, billing, and user roles."

User Management

Manage all user accounts across the platform — customers, staff, farm managers, and admins.

1
Search users — filter by name, email, or role. View total active users and role distribution.
2
Create users — User Management → Create User. Assign role and set initial password.
3
Edit roles — promote staff to Super Staff, downgrade roles, or deactivate accounts.
4
Force password reset — send a reset link for users who forgot credentials.
Real user roles:
• CUSTOMER — "Adebayo Johnson — Lagos, orders catfish weekly."
• FARM_MANAGER — "Dr. Ngozi Eze — oversees farm operations, batches, and feed."
• SUPER_STAFF — "Mr. Adeyemi — manages staff, shop inventory, and orders."
• SUPER_ADMIN — "TAMIPEE Founder — full control."

Activity Timeline

Audit logs track every significant action taken in the admin dashboard for accountability and debugging.

1
Review recent actions — see who created, updated, or deleted records and when.
2
Filter by user or action type — narrow down to a specific staff member or action (create, update, delete).
3
Trace issues — if an order status changed unexpectedly, the audit log shows who changed it and when.
