How to Use This Page

This page helps you compare your batches side-by-side so you can see which ones are performing best and worst — in terms of cost, growth, health, and profit. Use the charts below to quickly spot trends and patterns, then refer to the detailed table at the bottom for specific numbers. This way, you know what's working well and what needs attention.

Feed Cost Comparison

Shows which batch is consuming the most feed budget. Higher bars mean higher costs.

Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) Comparison

Lower is better — it measures how efficiently feed turns into body weight.

Mortality Rate Comparison

Shows death rate as a percentage per batch. Lower is better.

Profit Distribution

Each slice shows one batch's share of total farm profit.

Growth Rate Comparison

Shows how fast each batch gains weight per day. Faster is usually better, but balance with FCR.

Feed Cost Distribution

Shows each batch's share of total feed spend. Bigger slices mean higher feed costs.

Stock Distribution

Shows how your total livestock/fish count is split across batches.

Feed Cost Trend Over Time

Shows how monthly feed spending changes over time. Rising lines mean increasing costs.

Health & Vaccination Analytics

Vaccination Coverage per Batch

Shows how many vaccination records have been logged for each batch relative to its current animal count. Higher bars mean better coverage.

Health Issue Frequency per Batch

Shows the number of medication/health entries recorded for each batch. More entries mean more logged health issues.

Most Common Health Reasons

Shows which health issues are being treated most often across all batches.

Most Common Medications Used

Shows which medications are used most frequently across all health logs.
